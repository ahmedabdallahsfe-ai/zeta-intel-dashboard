Target Working Days = (Calendar Days − TOT [Time Out of Territory]) × tier multiplier (DM/DSM 0.8, ASM 0.6, NSM 0.3). Field Working Days % = All Visit Days / Target Working Days. Multipliers differ by tier by design, so raw % is not directly comparable across tiers -- shown side-by-side here for context only, not ranking.
