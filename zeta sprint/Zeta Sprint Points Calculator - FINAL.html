Points Calculator Guide

What is Zeta Sprint?

Zeta Sprint 2026 is your company-wide sales competition running from June to December. Every month you compete, earn points, and climb the leaderboard. The top performers each month, quarter, and year take home cash prizes and recognition at the CEO level.

How does scoring work?

Each role has its own KPI formula. Points are calculated using a non-linear curve — meaning the higher your achievement, the more points you earn per percentage point. Reaching 90% scores far more than double 45%.

Competition levels

  • Monthly Sprint — Top performers each month win cash + certificate
  • Quarterly Champion — Cumulative 3-month score, bigger prize + CEO Dinner
  • Annual Marathon — Full-year champion, the highest prize of all

Performance tiers

🏆 Champion — 88+ pts
🔥 Performer — 68–87 pts
📈 On Track — 42–67 pts
⚠️ Below Target — 0–41 pts

KPI thresholds — important!

Each KPI has a minimum threshold. Below that threshold you score zero on that KPI regardless of your other results:

  • MSR Sales: minimum 70% to start earning points
  • MSR Right Frequency: minimum 60%
  • MSR Coverage: minimum 90%
  • Field Working Days: minimum 60%
  • DV Coverage: minimum 80%
  • Avg Calls/DV: minimum 90%
